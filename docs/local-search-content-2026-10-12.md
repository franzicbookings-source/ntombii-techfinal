Newcastle remains the identified base. Osizweni and Madadeni are covered in useful service-specific requests, article summaries, local sections and metadata. Wider KZN enquiries remain possible through shared regional pages. Collection, transport, stock and on-site work are agreed per request. There are no new duplicate town pages or claimed branches.

The two new signage/printing images are image-generated branded design mockups; captions identify them as mockups. Real portfolio screenshots remain unchanged. The mockups are encoded as WebP at full 1536×1024 resolution for smaller downloads.

The Notes section uses a genuine WhatsApp enquiry link for tips. The previous form only changed local state and did not save a subscription; its misleading success state was removed.

The approved university/college/NSFAS fee reference was checked and the earlier conflicting amounts were corrected. Institution fees remain separate, and submission confirmation is distinguished from admission or funding approval.
