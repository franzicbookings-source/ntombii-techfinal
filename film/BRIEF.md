# Two Lights — Ntombii Tech brand film (60 s, 16:9)

## The story (verified)
Everything stated on screen comes from the About page and docs/about-team.md (owner-approved):
- An uncle and his niece, Sabelo and Sthembile Ndlovu, in Newcastle, KZN. Approved heading: "An uncle, his niece, and a laptop."
- Sabelo worked in the music industry, managing artists and booking their shows. After discovering AI tools he taught himself web development.
- He introduced Sthembile to computers and AI; she took to page layouts and graphics and now leads design.
- "Sabelo builds the websites; Sthembile designs the layouts and graphics." They build business and e-commerce websites for businesses across South Africa.
The owner asked to keep to the About story, to name no "first" project, and to keep the meaning of the name private. No founding motive is stated. Neither founder is depicted.

## Treatments considered
1. **Two Lights (chosen).** A warm stage light and a cool screen light meet over a laptop, hand light to each other, build a website together and end as the dot in the logo.
2. **The Setlist.** The film structured as a gig (opening act, soundcheck, headline), drawn from Sabelo's bookings background. Rejected: it makes the film his story, with Sthembile as a guest.
3. **Two Cursors, One Canvas.** Two multiplayer cursors build on one canvas. Rejected: reads as a software demo, not the opening of a short film.

Why Two Lights: light is physical, cinematic and faceless. Two lights can have personalities (steady and warm; quick, bouncy and cool), so the relationship plays out in timing. The warm light has a true origin (stage and shows) and the cool light has one too (screens, layouts). It carries the whole minute and lands on something real: the logo's dot.

## Format decisions
- No narration. The film must work as a silent preview on the website, and a synthetic voice telling a personal family story would feel less personal than the founders' own documented words set as type. All story beats are on-screen copy, so no captions file is needed.
- Copy is large enough to read on a phone (main lines ≥ 76 px at 1080p).
